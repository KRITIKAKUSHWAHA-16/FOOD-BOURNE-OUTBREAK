# University Foodborne Outbreak Investigation

## Overview

A simulated epidemiological investigation of a university
foodborne outbreak using R.

## Research Question

What happened during the outbreak, and which food exposure
was associated with illness?

## Dataset

The dataset contains 420 simulated university participants
and includes demographic characteristics, food exposures,
illness status, symptom onset dates, symptoms, illness
duration, and hospitalisation.

This dataset is simulated for educational and portfolio
purposes and does not contain real patient information.

## Methods

- Descriptive epidemiology
- Attack rates
- Person, place and time analysis
- Epidemic curve
- Food-specific attack rates
- Relative risk
- 95% confidence intervals
- Chi-square testing
- Stratified analysis

## Tools

- R
- RStudio
- dplyr
- ggplot2
- epidemiological measures

## Key Findings

- 420 participants were investigated.
- 98 participants developed illness.
- Overall attack rate: 23.3%.
- Cases were concentrated within a relatively short period.
- Food exposures were compared using relative risk and
  confidence intervals.

## Limitations

- The dataset is simulated.
- Exposure information is assumed to be accurately reported.
- Observational associations do not by themselves establish
  causation.
